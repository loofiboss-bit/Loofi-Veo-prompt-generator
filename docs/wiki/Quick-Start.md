# Quick Start

Get up and running with Loofi Creator Studio v13.0.0 in minutes.

## 1. Transpile a Multi-Model Video Prompt

1. Launch the app and navigate to **Prompt Studio → Video** (`/` or `/studio`).
2. Type your scene concept into the **Idea** field (e.g., _"A cybernetic courier rides a high-speed hoverbike through an illuminated neon rainstorm, tracking camera following alongside"_).
3. Select your **Model Target** (Google Veo 3.1, Kling 1.5/2.0, Runway Gen-3/4, OpenAI Sora, or Luma Ray-2).
4. (Optional) In **3D Camera & Optics**, choose your focal length (e.g., 35mm), aperture ($f/1.8$), and trajectory (e.g., Steadicam Follow).
5. Click **Optimize prompt**.
6. Review the three compiled variants: **Recommended Primary**, **Cinematic**, and **Control-Focused**.
7. Click **Copy Prompt** or **Copy All** to paste directly into your video generator.

## 2. Generate a Suno Lyrics Pack

1. Open `/studio?mode=music` or click **Music & Lyrics** in Prompt Studio.
2. Select your lyrics language (English, Swedish, Spanish, etc.) and vocal/instrumental mode.
3. Describe the musical genre, tempo, emotional mood, and lyrical story.
4. Click **Generate lyrics pack**.
5. Use **Rewrite section**, **Improve hook**, or **Lock Section** to fine-tune the stanzas.
6. Click **Copy Style**, **Copy Lyrics**, or **Copy & Open Suno** for a seamless manual handoff into Suno Custom Mode.

## 3. Direct with the AI Co-Director (Gemini Live)

1. Click the microphone icon on the Co-Director widget.
2. Speak your directing instruction (e.g., _"Set the camera to a low-angle 24mm lens and adjust the mood to stormy twilight"_).
3. Watch the Co-Director adjust scene parameters and camera rigs in real time.

## 4. Render Offline with ComfyUI Local GPU

1. Ensure your local ComfyUI instance is running (`http://127.0.0.1:8188`).
2. In **Production → Step 4 (Generate)**, choose **ComfyUI Local** as the provider.
3. Select your model pipeline (SVD-XT, HunyuanVideo, CogVideoX, or AnimateDiff).
4. Click **Generate Take** for zero-cost offline video rendering.

## 5. Collaborate in the Virtual Writers' Room

1. Navigate to `/hubs/collaboration`.
2. Click **Host Room** to generate a copyable LAN pairing code, or enter a peer's code to join.
3. Assign creative roles (Director, Cinematographer, Sound Designer, Screenwriter, Editor) and collaborate in real-time over your local network with zero cloud dependencies.
