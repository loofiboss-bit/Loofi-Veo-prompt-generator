# Review and revision

The Review step provides A/B playback, score breakdown, comparison notes, and Keep, Reject, or Revise actions. Local deterministic review runs first. Gemini semantic review is a separate explicit approval.

Omni interaction IDs are stored for follow-up revisions. Veo retakes remain separate paid submissions with a new ceiling.

## Studio draft revisions

For prompt text, use **Versions and comparison** in Prompt Studio rather than treating a paid retake
as a draft revision. **Save version** keeps both video and music workspace state. **Restore as new
version** preserves the draft being replaced. AI enhancement and lyric rewrites present proposals
for review before acceptance; locked sections remain exact.

See [Prompt Quality and Iteration](Prompt-Quality-and-Iteration.md) for checkpoints, readiness checks,
Model Arena and the complete template library. Draft revisions do not submit a new generation request.
